4. GENERAL TERMS AND CONDITIONS
4.1 Transfer of Ownership & Outstanding Fines

The motorcycle's logbook (V5C) will only be transferred to the Buyer upon receipt of the full payment of the outstanding balance. If there are any traffic offenses or fines issued in the Seller's name caused by the Buyer prior to the transfer, the ownership transfer will remain strictly on hold. The motorcycle will only be transferred after the full resolution of these fines (via direct payment, formal transfer of liability, or official nullification after a successful appeal).

4.2 Late Payments, Default & Vehicle Recovery

If an installment is not paid on the stipulated due date, the Buyer shall pay an additional late payment fee of £10.00 per day of delay. If the payment reaches the 6th (sixth) day of delay, the Seller reserves the right to immediately recover (collect) the motorcycle. In this event, the Buyer may only retrieve the motorcycle by paying the entire pending balance of the contract in full, in addition to any recovery costs incurred by the Seller.

4.3 Warranty Requirements

The Seller provides a warranty of 3 months or 3,000 miles (whichever comes first) strictly covering the engine and gearbox. To maintain this warranty, the Buyer must strictly follow the maintenance schedule and change the engine oil every 600 miles. There is no warranty on electrical or other parts, and breakdown recovery is not included.

4.4 Theft, Loss, or Damage

In the event of theft, total loss, or damage to the motorcycle, the risk lies entirely with the Buyer. The Buyer remains fully legally and financially responsible for settling all outstanding balances and obligations under this contract.

4.5 Validation of Documents

The Buyer hereby declares and warrants that all documents provided to the Seller for the execution of this contract—including but not limited to the Driving Licence, CBT (Compulsory Basic Training, if applicable), Insurance Certificate, Proof of Address, and any other requested documentation—are valid, authentic, and up-to-date.

4.6 Legal Compliance (Insurance, MOT, and Tax)

While the motorcycle remains registered in the Seller's name, the Buyer must ensure the vehicle is covered by valid Insurance, Road Tax, and an active MOT. If the motorcycle is found to be lacking any of these legal requirements at any point, the Seller reserves the right to immediately collect and withhold the motorcycle until the Buyer fully resolves the compliance issues.

4.7 Voluntary Return & Breach of Contract

In the event of a breach of contract, or if the Buyer voluntarily wishes to return the motorcycle, the Seller may (at its sole discretion) choose to buy the motorcycle back. This process is subject to a full mechanical evaluation and a new pricing assessment by the Seller to determine the current value before any financial agreement or settlement is made with the Buyer.

4.8 Accident Liability and Mandatory Claims

If the Buyer is involved in a road traffic accident while the motorcycle is still registered in the Seller's name, the Buyer bears total and exclusive responsibility for the accident, third-party damages, and any associated costs or liabilities. However, any insurance claim, accident management, or repair process resulting from such an accident MUST obligatorily be processed and managed directly through the Seller. The Seller shall not be held financially or legally responsible for the accident itself, but retains the exclusive right to manage the claim while holding the legal title of the vehicle.

4.9 Data Protection (UK GDPR)

The Seller collects, processes, and securely stores the Buyer’s personal data in strict compliance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018. The personal information and documentation provided by the Buyer (including identification, proof of address, and financial details) will be used solely for the purposes of executing this agreement, facilitating vehicle registration with the DVLA, legal compliance, and debt collection procedures if necessary. The Buyer consents to the processing of their data for these express purposes.

4.10 Indemnity

The Buyer agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Seller, its directors, and employees from and against any and all claims, liabilities, fines, penalties, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from or related to the Buyer’s possession, operation, maintenance, or use of the motorcycle. This includes, but is not limited to, traffic violations, parking fines, toll charges (e.g., ULEZ, Congestion Charge), towing fees, criminal acts, and any third-party claims for personal injury or property damage.

4.11 Governing Law and Jurisdiction

This Agreement and any dispute or claim arising out of, or in connection with it, its subject matter or formation (including non-contractual disputes or claims) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales. Both parties irrevocably agree that the courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute or claim that arises out of or in connection with this Agreement.
